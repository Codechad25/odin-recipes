Lasagna

Description

This classic homemade lasagna features layers of savory beef ragù, creamy cheese filling, and tender pasta sheets baked to golden perfection. It is a hearty, comforting dish perfect for family gatherings or weekend meal prep.

The secret to this recipe lies in the balance of rich, tomato-infused meat sauce and a velvety cheese layer that binds the structure together. Once baked, the top transforms into a beautiful, bubbly crust of melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • Olive oil
  • Ground beef
  • Chopped onions
  • Minced garlic
  • Crushed canned tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan and brown the ground beef with onions and garlic.
  2. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, salt, and pepper, then simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Mix the ricotta cheese with the beaten egg and a handful of Parmesan in a medium bowl.
  4. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a baking dish.
  5. Place a layer of pasta sheets over the sauce, followed by a layer of the ricotta mixture and a sprinkle of mozzarella.
  6. Repeat the layers of sauce, pasta, and cheese until the dish is full, finishing with a thick layer of mozzarella on top.
  7. Cover with foil and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes until bubbly.
  8. Rest for 15 minutes before slicing and serving.
